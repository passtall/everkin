# Gas explosion sound (1)

Played when poison gas explodes (game_system §12.2: fire meets gas, or Lightning Bolt or Chain Lightning hits a unit standing in it). Not made yet (2026-11-13). It joins the battle sounds in [sounds.md](sounds.md) and follows their format: OGG Vorbis 48 kHz mono, about −16 LUFS integrated, **3 variants** `gas_explode_1`, `_2`, `_3` in `assets/audio/battle/`. The existing sounds were synthesized by `tools/audio/build_audio.py` (sounds-notes.md); making this one the same way keeps the set consistent.

Style: soft storybook fantasy game sound, gentle and clean, no music, no melody, no voices, no reverb tail longer than the stated length, no clipping. Suitable for ages 7 and up: impacts are cushioned, never gory.
