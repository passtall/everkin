Battle screen mockup

A clickable sketch of the battle screen at the 1920 × 1080 base, built from the adopted rules and real creature art. It is a layout to react to, not finished styling. Try it: pick a skill, hover or tap an enemy to preview, click or tap the same enemy again to use it, and right-click or long-press any card to inspect it. Use the buttons above the screen to switch between the desktop layout and the phone layout. Version 2 applies your notes: no row labels, a vertical timeline on the left, a shorter Momentum bar, and one side at a time on phones. Then answer the review questions below.

Sample battle: Thornfang (Feral, Hunter) is acting; only its Hunter skills are shown so the bar fits one row. Targets, numbers and chances are illustrative.

What each area is
  • Timeline (left). The same height as the Momentum bar: the next 8 entries from top to bottom, scrollable for more. Your side has a green edge, the enemy a red one. Auto events (the dark entries) show their owner. Selecting a skill shows a dashed outline where the acting unit's next turn would land.
  • Board (middle). Enemy rows at the top, yours at the bottom, Front rows meeting at the battle line, no row labels. Compact cards show art, an HP bar and up to four statuses, then +N. On phones only one side shows at a time, with bigger cards and a button to switch, and the view flies smoothly across the battle line; picking an attack switches to the enemy side by itself.
  • Momentum (right edge, about 70% of its old height). −10 at the top (enemy) to +10 at the bottom (you). A skill with a cost shows a dashed marker where the meter lands after paying.
  • Skill bar (bottom). Icon space, short name, Delay (D) and Momentum cost (M). Move and Skip Turn sit apart at the right end.
  • Preview (floating). Damage range and the skill's success chance, plus which units may intercept.
  • Inspection. Front and back side by side. Keywords on the back explain themselves on hover or tap.
