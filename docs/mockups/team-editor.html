Team editor mockup

A clickable sketch of the deck-builder style team editor at the 1920 × 1080 base. Pick a saved team at the top, filter the collection on the right, drag a creature into a row to add it, drag placed creatures to move them or out of the formation to remove them, and right-click or press and hold any creature to inspect it. The night look is the adopted direction; add a note below if anything should change.

Drop here to remove from team

Sample collection: the first 30 creatures of the draft roster. Rows are labeled here because this is setup, not battle.

What each area is
  • Saved teams (top). Named teams with their formations. The last used one is preselected. Save keeps the current team under its name.
  • Formation (left). Your three rows as they stand in battle, Front nearest the enemy at the top. Drag creatures in, between rows, or out to remove them; a gold line shows where a creature will land.
  • Collection (right). Filter by class, size tier and attack type, sort by name, HP, Speed or Power. Creatures already in the team are dimmed.
  • Rules shown live. Up to six creatures, each at most once, up to six per row. The line under the teams says what is missing.
